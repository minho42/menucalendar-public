# Menu Calendar Privacy Policy

Menu Calendar does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- **Calendar access.** The app asks for access to your calendars so it can show today's events in the menu bar. Events are read on your Mac only and are never sent anywhere.
- **No internet.** The app has no network access and makes no network connections.
- **No tracking.** There are no accounts, analytics, ads or third-party services.
- **Settings.** Your chosen calendar and preferences are stored only on your Mac.

You can turn off calendar access at any time in **System Settings › Privacy & Security › Calendars**.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here.
